The honest scoreboard

BC Transit publishes a predicted arrival for every bus at every stop. bide records those predictions, then records when each bus actually arrived, and scores them on the same trips, stops and lead times as bide's own model.

The first honest comparison needs about two weeks of history. Until then this card shows what has been recorded so far, so nobody has to take the headline on trust.
